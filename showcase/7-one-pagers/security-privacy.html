Farmer's rights in the app (DPDP basics)

  • Consent first: simple Hindi consent screen with 🔊, before any data is used; asked again when the text changes.
  • "मेरा डेटा": download everything as a file, or delete it at once (two confirmations). Completed orders are anonymised, not deleted.
  • Complaints: in-app complaint queue, coordinator answers within 48 hours.
  • India's DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified on 14 Nov 2025 with an 18-month phased compliance timeline and rights to access, correct and erase data [S5]. Our privacy policy and terms are drafts awaiting legal review.

What the AI receives

  • Photo check: only the photo and the crop name (tested automatically).
  • Follow-up chat: crop, the earlier answer and the questions – never the photo or phone ID.
  • Selling: the product photo and the seller's words with phone numbers removed.

Security controls (built)

  • HTTPS only; login cookie HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, Secure.
  • PIN lockout after 5 wrong tries (15 min); rate limits on every API.
  • Strict Content-Security-Policy, helmet headers, every input validated.
  • Server checks every role and ownership (a seller can only change own listings; delivery links expire after 14 days or on delivery).
  • Audit log: every coordinator action (who, what, when) – append-only, cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Logs hide PIN, tokens, phone numbers and photos; error reports (optional) carry no user data.
  • Backups: daily, encrypted, kept 14 days (GitHub Actions or server cron; restore script included).
  • No known vulnerable packages (npm audit: high = 0); dependencies kept minimal.

Open items (honest)

  • Legal review of policy, terms, consent text and retention.
  • Hosting not yet live; server region planned Singapore (Render), database Mumbai (Atlas).
  • Google processes photos under its API terms; a government-hosted model can be connected if required.
  • No external security audit yet – welcome before scale-up.
