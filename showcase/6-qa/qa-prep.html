AI aur zimmedari

1AI galat salaah de de aur kisan ki fasal kharab ho jaaye to zimmedar kaun?

AI salaah deta hai, faisla kisan ka. Isliye app 4 cheezein karta hai: (1) photo saaf na ho ya AI ko bharosa kam ho to jawab hi nahi deta – nayi photo maangta hai; (2) dawai sirf generic naam se, kabhi exact matra ya brand nahi; (3) banned/khatarnaak dawaiyon ki list code mein block hai; (4) har jawab mein "KVK / krishi adhikari se pakka karein". Terms of use mein ye saaf likha hai (abhi legal review baaki). Pilot isi liye supervised hai – KVK expert sample jawab har 2 hafte check karega.

2AI kitna sahi hai? Accuracy kitni hai?

Abhi hum koi accuracy number nahi batate – kyunki asli Churu ke khet ki labelled photos par test nahi hua. Eval ka poora tarika bana hua hai (npm run eval). Pilot se pehle KVK expert ke saath 100 labelled photos par review hoga; pass hone ki shart: ≥70% sahi, unclear photo par 100% mana, banned dawai 0. Ye number tab hi batayenge.

3Kya AI banned pesticide suggest kar sakta hai?

Prompt mein mana hai, aur uske baad server par alag check hai: banned/restricted list (dawaiSafety.ts, inputRules.ts) se match hone wali dawai jawab se hata di jaati hai aur warning lagti hai. Ye automatic tests se pakka hai. List KVK expert se verify karwani baaki hai.

4Kaunsa AI use karte ho? Kal Google band kar de ya mehnga ho jaaye to?

Abhi Google Gemini. Saare AI calls ek hi interface ke peeche hain, isliye doosra model – Indian ya sarkari server par chalne wala – bina app badle lag sakta hai. AI band ho to bhi mausam, paani, bazaar, sanyojak ka kaam chalta rehta hai.

Data aur privacy

5Kisan ka data kahan rakha jaata hai? Desh ke bahar to nahi?

Database ka plan MongoDB Atlas ka Mumbai region hai. Server (Render) ka plan Singapore region hai – India ke paas sabse nazdeek; zarurat ho to India-hosted server par shift ho sakta hai. Jaanch ki photo hum store nahi karte; woh ek baar AI (Google) ko jaati hai. Abhi deploy live nahi hai – ye sab pilot se pehle tay hoga.

6AI company ko kisan ki pehchaan milti hai?

Nahi. Photo jaanch mein sirf photo aur fasal ka naam jaata hai; chat mein na photo, na phone ID; bechne ke vaakya se phone number hata diya jaata hai. Ye automatic tests se check hota hai. Phone photo chhoti karte samay GPS data bhi hat jaata hai.

7DPDP Act ka paalan kaise?

Pehle sahmati (Hindi + 🔊), maksad saaf, kam se kam data, "मेरा डेटा" se download aur turant delete, 14 din mein backup expire, audit log. DPDP Rules 14 Nov 2025 ko notify hue aur 18 mahine ka phased timeline hai [S5]. Policy/terms abhi draft hain – vakil ka review pilot se pehle zaroori hai.

8Data bechoge? Ad dikhaoge?

Nahi. Privacy policy mein likha hai: data na bechte hain, na ad ke liye use karte hain. Kamai ka model sirf poore order par chhota commission hai.

Kisan tak pahunch

9Jo kisan padh-likh nahi sakte, woh kaise chalayenge?

Photo-pehle design, bade button, har screen par 🔊 padhkar sunana, peela mic se bolkar chalana ("मौसम बताओ"), aur SHG didi ki madad ("मदद से लिस्टिंग" – didi kisan ki sahmati se uska maal daal sakti hai). Ye sab bana hua hai; asli kisanon par kitna kaam karta hai, ye pilot mein naapenge.

10Gaon mein internet kam hai. Tab?

App halka hai (pehli load ~146 KB JS gzip), photo bhejne se pehle phone par chhoti hoti hai, aur purani jaankari (mausam, salaah, jaanch) bina internet ke dikhti hai. Nayi photo jaanch ke liye network chahiye. Kis gaon mein kitna chalta hai – pilot mein dekhenge.

11Har kisan ke paas smartphone nahi hai.

Sahi baat. Isliye SHG didi ka role hai – woh apne phone se kai kisanon ki madad kar sakti hai. Feature phone/IVR version abhi nahi bana; pilot ke baad zarurat dekh kar.

12Hindi to theek, Marwari/Shekhawati bolne wale?

Hindi poora hai; Rajasthani ka draft hai (menu aur "जवाब राजस्थानी में") jo local logon se padhwa kar sudharna baaki hai. Voice ke keywords mein kuch Marwari shabd hain ("मेह", "पाणी").

Kharcha aur tikaav

13Chalane ka kharcha kitna hai?

Pilot (50 kisan) ke liye anumaan, jo asli bill se pakka hoga: AI – Gemini ka free tier hai; paid par Gemini 3.8 Flash $0.75/10 lakh input aur $3.75/10 lakh output token (31 Dec 2026 tak) [S6]. Ek jaanch ~3,500 input + ~1,500 output token maan kar ≈ $0.008 (≈ ₹0.7, ₹88/$ maan kar); 1,000 jaanch ≈ $8. Database: Atlas M0 free (512 MB), aage Flex $8/mahina se [S8]. Server: Render free tier production ke liye nahi hai [S7], isliye paid instance lagega (render.com par daam). Ye sab ANUMAAN hai.

14Paisa kahan se aayega? Free kab tak?

Kisan ke liye salaah free. Kamai: poore order par 2–5% commission (abhi 3%) aur input dealer ka referral. Pilot ka maksad yahi dekhna hai ki ye kharcha nikaalta hai ya nahi – abhi koi daava nahi.

15Pilot ke baad kaun chalayega?

Fisto Up team. Code dastavez ke saath hai (README, DEPLOY, checklist), tests automatic hain, aur zarurat ho to sarkari/NIC server par bhi chal sakta hai.

Sarkari apps aur scale

16Sarkar ke apps pehle se hain (Kisan Suvidha, KCC, Rajasthan ke portal). Ye kyun?

Hum unki jagah lena nahi chahte. Fark: ek hi jagah photo se bimari jaanch + mausam/paani ki salaah + bechna + mandi gaadi, Hindi aawaz ke saath, aur gaon ki SHG didi ka panel. App sarkari yojanaon ki jaankari aur official link dikhata hai, mandi bhaav data.gov.in se le sakta hai. Jahan sarkari system behtar hai, wahan link dena hi sahi hai.

17Rajasthan ke saare zilon mein kaise failega?

Pehle ek zile (Churu) mein supervised pilot. Scale ke liye: har zile ke KVK se niyam (fasal calendar, paani, alert) verify, har zile mein SHG sanyojak, aur server/AI ka kharcha usage ke hisaab se. Tech ek instance se shuru hai; badhne par Redis rate-limit aur bada database chahiye (DEPLOY.md mein likha hai). Bina pilot data ke scale ka daava nahi.

18KVK aur krishi vibhag se kya rishta hai?

Abhi koi official partnership nahi. App har jawab mein KVK se poochhne ko kehta hai, aur saare niyam "verify with KVK" mark hain. Rajasthan mein 47 KVK hain [S4]. Hum chahte hain ki pilot mein Churu KVK expert review aur niyam verify karein.

Bazaar, dhokha aur SHG

19Bazaar mein dhokha ho to? Nakli seller, kharab maal?

Seller tabhi bech sakta hai jab SHG didi verify kare (phone par baat, ₹1 UPI check, dawai dealer ka licence). Har order par shikayat, 48 ghante mein sanyojak ka jawab, faisla dono ko dikhta hai; 90 din mein 3 sahi shikayat par seller "बार-बार शिकायत" mark hota hai. Coordinator ka har kaam audit log mein.

20Paise app mein atakte hain? Payment gateway?

Nahi. Buyer seedhe seller ko UPI ya nakad deta hai. App mein online payment nahi hai (jaan-boojhkar) – paise ka risk app par nahi aata.

21SHG didi ka role kya hai? Unhe kya milega?

Seller verify, madad se listing, orders/delivery par nazar, shikayat, mandi gaadi, commission ka hisaab. Unke liye Hinglish training manual aur roz ka checklist bana hai. Didi ko kitna maandey milega – pilot mein SHG ke saath tay hoga; abhi koi vaada nahi.

22Pashu bikri – kanoon ka kya?

Pashu listing coordinator ki manzoori ke baad hi dikhti hai aur app kanooni notice dikhata hai. Rajasthan ke oont aur govansh niyamon ka legal review baaki hai; tab tak pilot mein ye suvidha band rakh sakte hain (feature flag).

Sarkar se kya chahiye

23Aapko sarkar se kya chahiye?

Paisa nahi maang rahe. Chahiye: (1) Churu zile mein 4 hafte ka supervised pilot ki anumati; (2) Churu KVK ke expert ka samay – 100 photo review aur niyam verify; (3) Rajeevika/SHG ke 2–3 group se jod; (4) pilot ke nateeje zila prashasan ke saath share karna.

24Nateeje kaise pata chalenge? Kaun naapega?

Pilot dashboard (sirf ginti, kisi kisan ka naam nahi): sakriya kisan, jaanch, 👍/👎, "क्या फसल ठीक हुई?", listing, order, bikri, kisan ka bhaav banaam mandi bhaav. Har hafte review sheet, aur pehle se likhe stop/continue niyam (pilot plan). KVK ka sample check alag se.

25Pilot fail ho gaya to?

Niyam pehle se likhe hain: banned dawai ki salaah ya data leak par turant band. Agar sakriya kisan 25% se kam ya KVK ka 'sahi' 70% se kam – rukte hain aur sochte hain. Fail hona bhi ek seekh hai; hum nateeje imaandari se share karenge.
