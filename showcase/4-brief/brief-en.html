For the Government of Rajasthan

A Hindi farm assistant on the farmer’s own phone – supported by village SHGs

AgriAI helps a smallholder farmer check a sick crop from a photo, know when to water, get weather warnings, and sell produce – in simple Hindi, by touch or by voice. It is built and tested with demo data. We request a supervised 4-week pilot in Churu district with KVK and SHG support.

The problem in Rajasthan

  • Rajasthan grew 45.40% of India’s rapeseed & mustard and 14.66% of India’s nutri/coarse cereals in 2023-24 [S1]. Losses on these farms matter for the whole country.
  • 219 of Rajasthan’s 302 groundwater assessment units were over-exploited in the 2022 assessment [S2]. Every unnecessary irrigation counts.
  • Pests and diseases destroy up to 40% of global crop production each year [S3]. Early, correct identification makes the difference.
  • Expert advice exists – Rajasthan has 47 Krishi Vigyan Kendras [S4] – but a farmer often needs an answer in the field, the same day, in their own language.

Safety first

  • Refuses to guess: if the photo is unclear, not a plant, or the AI is unsure, the app asks for a new photo – no answer is shown.
  • Medicine blocklist: banned and restricted pesticides are removed by a server check after the AI; only generic names, never an exact dose.
  • KVK line on every answer: “confirm with your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra”.
  • Accuracy: not yet measured on field photos. A KVK expert review of 100 labelled photos is planned before the pilot (pass mark: ≥70% correct, 100% of unclear photos refused, 0 banned medicines).

Data privacy

  • The AI receives only the photo and the crop name – never a name, phone number or location (automatically tested). Check photos are not stored.
  • Consent screen in simple Hindi with read-aloud; “मेरा डेटा” lets a farmer download or delete their data at once; append-only audit log of coordinator actions; encrypted backups kept 14 days.
  • Designed around India’s DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified 14 Nov 2025, 18-month phased compliance) [S5]. Policy and terms are drafts awaiting legal review.

How it can support existing government efforts

  • KVKs: every answer points farmers to the KVK; all thresholds are open for KVK experts to verify and correct.
  • SHGs (e.g. Rajeevika groups): a defined role for SHG members as village coordinators (compensation to be agreed with the SHGs).
  • Schemes: plain-Hindi scheme information that links to the official website; it does not process applications.
  • Public data: can show mandi prices from data.gov.in. No official partnership exists today.
